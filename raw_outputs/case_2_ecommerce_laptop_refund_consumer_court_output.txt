=== NYAYAMITRA RAW OUTPUT FOR CASE_2_ECOMMERCE_LAPTOP_REFUND_CONSUMER_COURT ===
CATEGORY: consumer_protection_refund_deficiency
STANDING: MODERATE
SUMMARY: Refusal to refund or replace defective merchandise constitutes 'Deficiency of Service' under Section 2(11) and an 'Unfair Trade Practice' under Section 2(47) of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

=== STATUTORY ACTION ROADMAP ===
Step 1: Send the drafted pre-litigation Legal Demand Notice to the e-commerce platform's Grievance Officer and seller.
Step 2: Register a grievance on the National Consumer Helpline portal (consumerhelpline.gov.in / dial 1915) citing the notice.
Step 3: If unresolved within 15 days, file a formal complaint online via e-Daakhil (edaakhil.nic.in) before the District Consumer Commission without hiring an advocate.

1. STATEMENT OF FACTS & CHRONOLOGY:
1. On 12 October 2024, I purchased an Asus Vivobook laptop worth Rs 48,000 from Flipkart under Order OD328919102

2. When delivered on 15 October, the package was unsealed and the laptop display screen was completely shattered

2. STATUTORY VIOLATIONS & LEGAL GROUNDS:
Your aforesaid act of arbitrarily and unilaterally withholding the legitimate dues of the Undersigned is illegal, fraudulent, and amounts to actionable default under the laws of India, including:
• Violation of Consumer Protection Act, 2019 [Section 2(11) - Deficiency of Service]: Any fault, imperfection, shortcoming or inadequacy in the quality, nature and manner of performance required by law or contract.
• Violation of Consumer Protection Act, 2019 [Section 2(47) - Unfair Trade Practice]: Deceptive practices including refusing refunds for defective goods, false guarantees, or charging above agreed price.
• Violation of Consumer Protection Act, 2019 [Section 35 - Filing of Complaint before District Commission (e-Daakhil)]: Any consumer can file a complaint directly online via e-Daakhil for claims up to ₹50 Lakhs without needing to hire an advocate.
• Violation of Limitation Act, 1963 [Article 113 / General Schedule - Statutory Period of Limitation for Recovery]: General civil recovery suits must be filed within 3 years from the date payment became due. Consumer complaints have a 2-year limitation (Section 69 CPA).

In such event, you shall be held liable not only for the principal claim and enhanced interest, but also for comprehensive damages for mental agony, harassment, and all legal costs incurred.
